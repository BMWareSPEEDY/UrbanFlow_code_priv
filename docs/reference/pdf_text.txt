4.  Mathematical  &  Physics  Framework  
4.1  Graph  Representation  
We  define  the  urban  surface  as  a  directed  spatial  graph  G  =  (V,  E),  where:  
●  V  =  {v1,  v2,  ...,  vN}  represents  spatial  nodes  (catchment  sub-areas,  road  intersections,  
building
 
perimeter
 
entry
 
points).
 ●  E  =  {(vi,  vj)}  represents  spatial  edges  carrying  surface  or  channel  water  flow  (roads,  open  
drains,
 
elevation
 
contours).
 
4.2  Governing  Hydrological  Equations  (Physics  Loss  Constraints)  
To  ensure  the  neural  network  obeys  physical  laws  rather  than  hallucinating  water  accumulation,  
the
 
loss
 
function
 
incorporates
 
the
 
1D
 
Saint-Venant
 
Shallow
 
Water
 
Equations:
 
1.  Continuity  Equation  (Conservation  of  Mass):  ∂A/∂t  +  ∂Q/∂x  =  q_in  2.  Momentum  Equation  (Conservation  of  Energy):  S_f  =  S_0  -  ∂y/∂x  -  (u/g)·(∂u/∂x)  -  
(1/g)·(∂u/∂t)
 3.  PINN  Loss  Formulation:  Loss_total  =  Loss_data  +  λ1  ·  Loss_mass  +  λ2  ·  Loss_momentum
